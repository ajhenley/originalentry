What the app does and does not do

It refuses, it does not hint
A refusal is always mechanical: an entry that does not balance, a journal without a column for that account, a deadline that has passed. It never comments on the accounts you chose or the figures you entered.
It keeps what you type
Entries are saved as you record them, and kept in this browser as well, so a dropped connection does not cost you an entry.
It works out only what follows
The ledgers, the trial balance and the totals are derived from your own entries and postings. Everything else on these screens is yours.
Your figures are your own
Companies come in versions with different figures, so a classmate's numbers will not match yours even where the method is identical.

When you are stuck

Read the document again before changing an entry; most of what looks like an app problem is a detail on the paper. The Reference holds this company's chart of accounts. After that, ask your instructor: they can see where you are and what the app last refused.
